Questions

About Extension Removal

Why should extensions be removed professionally?+

Careful professional removal helps protect your natural hair and prepares it for a refresh, reapplication or break.

How much does extension removal cost?+
Tape Extension Removal starts from $60 and K-Tip Extension Removal starts from $100. Pricing may vary with the amount of extensions and time required.
Can I plan my next extension appointment at the same time?+

Yes. Tell Rachel whether you are considering a refresh, reapplication or a break so the next step can be planned around your hair.
