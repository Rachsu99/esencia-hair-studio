Good to know

Before you book

How much does Keratin smoothing cost?+

Pricing starts from $180 and varies with hair length, thickness and the amount of product required. Final pricing is confirmed following consultation.

How much does Nanoplasty cost?+

Pricing starts from $280 and varies with hair length, thickness and product requirements. Your consultation confirms the most suitable option and final price.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?+

Start with a consultation. Rachel can assess your hair, listen to your desired finish and guide you toward the most suitable service.

How long will my appointment take?+

Appointment times vary by hair length, thickness and service. Esencia will confirm the expected time when your booking is arranged.

Should I wash my hair before my appointment?+

Preparation can vary by service. Please ask Esencia when booking so you receive the right advice for your appointment.
