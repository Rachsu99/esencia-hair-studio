Aftercare

Keep your results looking beautiful.

For best results, please avoid washing your hair for 48 hours after your Keratin treatment. We also recommend using sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner to help maintain your smooth, glossy finish.

Keratin smoothing FAQs

How long will my results last?+

Results can last up to 3 months with the right aftercare.

Is Keratin permanent?+

No. Keratin smoothing gradually fades over time, leaving your natural hair texture behind.

Is it suitable for coloured hair?+

Suitability will be checked during your consultation so the service can be tailored to your hair.

How long does the treatment take?+

Most appointments take approximately 1–2 hours depending on hair length and thickness.
